Phishing Threat Matrix & Red Flag Indicators

Comprehensive guide to key threat vectors, psychological cues, and link deception tactics.

📧
Sender Address & Domain Spoofing
  • Lookalike domains (typosquatting like paypaI.com with uppercase i).
  • Free webmail providers (using @gmail.com for official corporate notices).
  • Display name spoofing where friendly name contradicts sender address.
  • Header mismatches between From: and Reply-To: fields.
🔗
Hyperlink Deception & Masking
  • Anchor text displays a legitimate URL while underlying link points to malicious host.
  • Raw IP addresses used in links (e.g., http://192.168.1.1/login).
  • Suspicious low-cost top-level domains (.xyz, .info, .top).
  • URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl) used to obfuscate final destination.
🧠
Psychological Triggers & Phrasing
  • Extreme urgency (e.g., "Account suspended within 2 hours").
  • Fear tactics and monetary penalties ("$150 charge applied unless verified").
  • Greed / unexpected rewards ("Claim your $500 Amazon gift card").
  • Authority exploitation (Impersonating CEO or Law Enforcement).
📎
Malicious Payloads & Attachments
  • Executable files disguised as documents (e.g., Invoice_2026.pdf.exe).
  • Macro-enabled Office documents (.docm, .xlsm).
  • Password-protected ZIP/RAR archives designed to bypass gateway virus scanners.
  • Fake DocuSign / PDF preview links forcing credential entry.
